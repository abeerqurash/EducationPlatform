# Scope and product evidence

The requested next bundle prioritizes a usable public journey: discover a working tool, understand its method, make a study/application plan and move into existing authenticated practice.

Reviewed [Test Ninjas public home](https://test-ninjas.com/) on 10 October 2026. Its public navigation connects guides, exam products, admissions, pricing and account entry. Its marketing describes goal-based discovery and a trial-to-paid journey. These are public claims; authenticated features, score gains and paid entitlements were not verified. This release uses original learning content and avoids reproducing their questions or marketing claims.

The [LifeTrack reference](https://lifetrack-template.webflow.io/home) could not be retrieved by the web tool in this run. New public pages reuse the repository's established color, type, spacing, button and card tokens. Mobile screenshots were visually inspected; no fresh pixel-level comparison against that external reference is claimed.

## Inputs, outputs and behavior

Study planner inputs: whole weekly minutes, subject names and priority weights. Output: a deterministic whole-minute allocation whose total matches the budget; downloadable text. Largest-remainder rounding awards ties in input order. It does not save to an account, invent deadlines or make automatic performance claims.

Checklist inputs: eight completion toggles. Output: progress, downloaded text and optional local-browser storage. Saving requires an explicit action. Corrupted saved data is rejected without overwriting current selections. It does not infer admission chances or institutional eligibility.

Guides connect core grade/math calculations, study methods, illustrative practice interpretation and application organization. They use timeless worked examples and defer institutional requirements to official sources rather than publishing unverified deadlines or examination policies.

Public pages link to current dashboards and reviewed practice with account entry handled by existing authentication. They show present free access without fabricated premium entitlements. Search query pages are canonicalized to their directory and marked noindex; private routes are omitted from the sitemap. Robots rules are discovery guidance, not access control.

## Remaining completion work

1. Account verification and recovery: token lifecycle, abuse controls, configured transactional delivery and authenticated acceptance.
2. Billing/monetization: selected payment provider and currency, checkout, idempotent verified webhooks, enforced entitlements, consent-based analytics and advertising.
3. Learning scale: more reviewed original questions, passages/figures, full test definitions, question timing and calibrated reporting.
4. Parent/educator/school products: consent/linkage, assignments/cohorts and tenant isolation.
5. Production operations: database-package strict TypeScript debt from prior batches, deployment/operator policies, backups, monitoring, accessibility review and performance acceptance.

This release closes a concrete public feature set. It does not establish that the broad master specification is complete.
