# Product benchmark and remaining roadmap

Reviewed Test Ninjas public home, SAT and ACT pages on 10 October 2026:
[Home](https://test-ninjas.com/), [SAT](https://test-ninjas.com/sat),
[ACT](https://test-ninjas.com/act). These are public marketing descriptions, not an
independent verification of their scoring claims or authenticated workflows.

Its SAT page describes adaptive tests, interactive lessons, continuous practice,
topic-level reports and pacing analysis. The ACT page describes exam practice with
courses and tools. Their public pages promote a trial-to-paid journey. Exact
authenticated plan restrictions were not inspected. Do not reproduce their
proprietary questions, tutor explanations or score-improvement marketing claims.

User problem: trustworthy questions must move from authoring to review to practice,
with understandable results that remain stable after edits. This bundle adds our own
editorial source/version history, server-side answer handling and recoverable draft
sessions. It is free account practice within the current application; no subscription
gating is introduced before a billing/entitlement implementation exists.

Inputs: authenticated author content and provenance, independent review notes;
student exam/topic selection and answer indices. Outputs: published revisions,
educational percentages/topic breakdowns, saved answers, recoverable drafts and
private historical explanations. Official scaled scores and adaptive mastery are
not derived from these illustrative questions.

Discovery: admin sidebar and practice-home links connect the workflow. Private
editorial/student library pages are noindex. Public SEO content and related-guide
architecture remain a separate release. The new components reuse existing themed
controls; this is not a new pixel-level LifeTrack audit.

## Fastest meaningful next bundles

1. Public product/SEO: real tools directory entry point, sitemap/robots/canonical
   policies, useful guide/content pages, legal and support routes, contextual links
   and broken-link acceptance. Avoid filling missing pages with empty placeholders.
2. Account completion: transactional email abstraction and a configured delivery
   adapter, verification/reset workflows, abuse controls and authenticated browser tests.
3. Monetization: consent-first first-party events, provider sandbox checkout,
   entitlement enforcement and verified idempotent webhooks. Provider credentials and
   supported currencies must be selected before activation.
4. Learning completeness: more original reviewed content, passages/figures, full-length
   test definitions, per-question timing and evidence-based topic mastery.
5. Role products: parent linkage/consent, educator cohorts/assignments and tenant-isolated
   school operations; then localization/API/white-label expansion.

The 254 existing strict database-package TypeScript diagnostics also remain a release
quality task. New question-bank files have no diagnostics under that check. No overall
completion percentage is claimed; each bundle should have executed acceptance evidence.
